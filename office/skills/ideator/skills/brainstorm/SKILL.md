# Brainstorm

## Diverge
Write down at least ten raw ideas quickly, without judging them. Push for range by asking:
- **Who** has a problem with this topic? Beginners, experts, teams, people with little time or money.
- **When** does the problem hurt? Before, during, after; daily versus once a year.
- **What form** could help? A CLI, a web page, a browser extension, a bot, a dashboard, an API, a script.
- **What twist** would make it stand out? Offline, private, instant, playful, automated, social.
- What would the opposite of the obvious solution look like?

## Converge
1. Drop ideas that are vague, need data or access the developer cannot get, or already exist everywhere with nothing new.
2. Merge near-duplicates. The final ideas must differ in user, problem or form, not just in name.
3. Keep 3 to 5. Include at least one small idea that can be finished in one sitting.
4. For each, check: can the developer show something working at the end of one session? If not, cut it down to the part that can, and mark the scope honestly.
5. Recommend the idea with the best value for its effort, not the most ambitious one.
