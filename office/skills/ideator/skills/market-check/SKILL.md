# Market check

Keep it short: a few searches, not a research report.

1. For each candidate idea, search for the obvious existing solutions (one or two queries such as "<problem> app" or "<task> open source tool").
2. Note what the best existing options do well and what users still complain about: price, complexity, privacy, missing platform, missing feature.
3. Keep an idea only if it has a clear difference from what exists, and put that difference in its `why`.
4. If an idea is fully covered by a free, popular tool, drop it or change it until it is not.
5. Do not invent facts. If you could not check something, say "not checked" rather than guessing.
